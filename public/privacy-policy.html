Cleankr Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 | Effective Date: September 2026

Cleankr ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Cleankr mobile application, providing professional doorstep home cleaning, bathroom cleaning, kitchen deep cleaning, and appliance maintenance services across Pune and surrounding areas. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you").

Key Commitments at a Glance

  • No Third-Party Advertising: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or phone number to marketing agencies or advertisers.
  • Doorstep Safety PIN: Your booking is protected with a unique 4-digit PIN verified upon partner arrival.
  • Full Account & Data Deletion: You retain complete ownership and can request complete deletion of your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver quality cleaning services at your doorstep, Cleankr collects the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number (used for secure OTP login and booking communication), and optional email address for invoices.
  • Service Addresses: House/flat number, apartment/society name, street address, landmark, city (Pune), and PIN code to dispatch cleaning professionals to the exact location.
  • Precise & Approximate Location: Accessed only when you explicitly select "Use Current Location" to detect your address easily. Cleankr does NOT track location in the background.
  • Booking & Service Data: Selected services, add-ons, scheduled date and time slots, customer preferences, and ratings/reviews.
  • Doorstep Safety PIN: Automatically generated random 4-digit PIN for each booking to authenticate verified service partners.
  • Device & Notification Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to provide notifications regarding booking confirmations, cleaner assignment, arrival updates, and invoice generation.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is collected strictly to support and fulfill your cleaning requests:

  • Assigning and dispatching certified cleaning professionals from your nearest Pune Service Hub.
  • Verifying your identity securely via SMS OTP without requiring complex passwords.
  • Providing real-time service tracking, technician arrival alerts, and safety PIN validation.
  • Customer care resolution, quality follow-ups, and complimentary revisit scheduling.
  • Generating digital service receipts and booking history.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your personal data with any third parties except under the following strictly necessary circumstances:

  • Assigned Service Partners: The specific cleaner assigned to your service receives your customer name, phone number, and service address solely for reaching your premises and completing the requested service.
  • Infrastructure & Hosting Providers: We use Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging) hosted on secure Google Cloud servers to store and process booking transactions with TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Legal Requirements: Disclosures made only if required by applicable Indian laws or government authorities.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement stringent industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmissions between the Cleankr mobile application and our servers are encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • Database records are protected by granular Firestore Security Rules preventing unauthorized cross-user access.
  • Sensitive information is stored in compliant Google Cloud Platform facilities.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy

We retain your profile and booking records only for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services and comply with legal requirements.

User Right to Delete Account & Personal Data

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, any user can request the complete and permanent deletion of their account, saved addresses, and personal profile data at any time.

To request deletion, simply:

  • Send an email from your registered email address to cleankarservice@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Cleankr Account" including your registered phone number.
  • Or contact in-app Help & Support.

Upon verification, all your personal information will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

6. Permissions Requested & Why

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used strictly with foreground consent to auto-detect your cleaning service address.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to send real-time service updates, partner dispatch alerts, and doorstep PIN verification.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to communicate with our secure backend services and sync booking statuses.

7. Children's Privacy

Cleankr services are directed towards adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
